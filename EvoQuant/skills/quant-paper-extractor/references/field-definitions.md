# Field Definitions for Quant Research Report Extraction

Every JSONL record must contain exactly these 11 fields. No extra fields are allowed (except `_warnings` for metadata).

## Fields

### paperId
- **Type:** `str`
- **Required:** Yes
- **Word limit:** N/A (auto-generated)
- **Description:** SHA-256 hex digest of the PDF file's binary content. Also used as the filename stem for both the markdown file and the JSONL file.
- **Evidence rule:** Auto-generated by `compute_paper_id()`. No extraction needed.

### title
- **Type:** `str`
- **Required:** Yes
- **Word limit:** ≤ 30 words
- **Description:** English title of the research report. If the original title is in Chinese, translate it to English while preserving the meaning.
- **Evidence rule:** Must quote the original title from the document cover page or first page. If Chinese, provide the English translation.
- **Example:** `"Factor Momentum in A-Share Market: Evidence from 2010–2024"`

### year
- **Type:** `int`
- **Required:** Yes
- **Word limit:** 4 digits
- **Description:** Publication year of the report.
- **Evidence rule:** Must be found in the document date field, cover page, or footer. If not found, use the file modification year as fallback and flag in `_warnings`.
- **Example:** `2024`

### source
- **Type:** `str`
- **Required:** Yes
- **Word limit:** ≤ 10 words
- **Description:** English name of the securities/investment firm or research institution that published the report.
- **Evidence rule:** Must appear in the document header, footer, cover page, or logo area. Common sources: CITIC Securities, Huatai Research, Guotai Junan, CICC, China Merchants Securities, etc.
- **Example:** `"CITIC Securities"`

### keywords
- **Type:** `list[str]`
- **Required:** Yes
- **Item count:** 3–8
- **Description:** Quantitative finance keywords relevant to the report's content. Each keyword must be a term actually used or discussed in the document.
- **Evidence rule:** Each keyword must appear in the document text or be a direct English translation of a Chinese term that appears. **Do not infer keywords that are not grounded in the text.** If information is not explicitly found, return empty list and do not fabricate keywords.
- **Example:** `["factor momentum", "A-share", "alpha decay", "portfolio optimization", "long-short"]`

### tldr
- **Type:** `str`
- **Required:** Yes
- **Word limit:** ≤ 40 words
- **Description:** One-sentence summary of the core finding or recommendation.
- **Evidence rule:** Must paraphrase a specific claim from the document. Do not fabricate findings. If information is not explicitly found, return empty string.
- **Example:** `"Factor momentum generates significant alpha in A-share market with annualized return of 15.2% and Sharpe ratio of 1.8 from 2010 to 2024."`

### abstract
- **Type:** `str`
- **Required:** Yes
- **Word limit:** ≤ 150 words
- **Description:** Concise summary covering: (1) research question, (2) approach, (3) main conclusion.
- **Evidence rule:** Must be grounded in the document's executive summary or abstract section. Do not fabricate conclusions. If information is not explicitly found, return empty string.
- **Example:** `"This study investigates factor momentum in the A-share market from 2010 to 2024. Using a cross-sectional momentum strategy based on 6-month formation and 1-month holding periods, we find that factor momentum generates statistically significant alpha after controlling for market risk. The strategy achieves an annualized return of 15.2% with a Sharpe ratio of 1.8. Robustness checks confirm the result holds across different market regimes and transaction cost assumptions."`

### strategy
- **Type:** `str`
- **Required:** Yes
- **Word limit:** ≤ 300 words
- **Description:** The trading or investment strategy proposed in the report. Must include: what the strategy does, when it triggers, and what positions it takes.
- **Evidence rule:** Must include at least one inline evidence citation: `[source: "quoted or paraphrased text from document"]`. Do not fabricate strategy details. If information is not explicitly found, return empty string.
- **Example:** `"Long-short factor momentum strategy: go long the top decile of factors ranked by past 6-month returns and short the bottom decile, rebalanced monthly. [source: \"构建多空组合，做多过去6个月收益率最高的前10%因子，做空后10%\"]"`

### method
- **Type:** `str`
- **Required:** Yes
- **Word limit:** ≤ 300 words
- **Description:** The quantitative methodology used: models, signals, factor construction, backtesting framework, statistical tests.
- **Evidence rule:** Must include at least one inline evidence citation. Do not fabricate methodology. If information is not explicitly found, return empty string.
- **Example:** `"Cross-sectional regression with Newey-West adjusted t-statistics. Factor returns are constructed using sorted portfolio approach with 10 groups. Backtesting uses daily frequency data from Wind database, covering all A-share stocks excluding ST and newly listed stocks within 60 days. [source: \"采用排序法构建因子收益，分为10组，使用Newey-West调整t统计量\"]"`

### experiment
- **Type:** `str`
- **Required:** Yes
- **Word limit:** ≤ 300 words
- **Description:** The experimental setup: data range, stock universe, frequency, benchmarks, transaction cost assumptions, key parameters.
- **Evidence rule:** Must include at least one inline evidence citation. Do not fabricate experimental details. If information is not explicitly found, return empty string.
- **Example:** `"Data: Wind database, all A-share stocks, 2010-01 to 2024-06. Universe: excludes ST stocks and stocks listed within 60 days. Frequency: monthly rebalancing. Benchmark: CSI 300 Index. Transaction cost: 0.3% round-trip. Key parameters: formation period = 6 months, holding period = 1 month. [source: \"回测区间2010年1月至2024年6月，月度调仓，双边交易成本0.3%\"]"`

### result
- **Type:** `str`
- **Required:** Yes
- **Word limit:** ≤ 200 words
- **Description:** Key performance metrics and findings. Must include at least one specific numeric metric.
- **Evidence rule:** Must include at least one specific numeric metric (return %, Sharpe ratio, drawdown %, etc.) that appears in the document. Do not fabricate numbers. If information is not explicitly found, return empty string.
- **Example:** `"Long-short portfolio annualized return: 15.2%, Sharpe ratio: 1.8, maximum drawdown: 8.3%. Long-only portfolio annualized return: 22.1%, Sharpe ratio: 1.2. Alpha remains significant after Fama-French 3-factor adjustment (t=3.45). Strategy underperforms in bull market regimes but provides hedging in downturns. [source: \"多空组合年化收益15.2%，夏普比率1.8，最大回撤8.3%\"]"`

## General Rules

1. **All output must be in English.** Translate any Chinese source text.
2. **Quote-or-zero rule:** For `strategy`, `method`, `experiment`, `result`, every claim must include an inline evidence citation `[source: "..."]`. No citation → flag as warning.
3. **No fabrication.** If information is not explicitly found in the document, return empty string (`""`) or empty list (`[]`). Do not infer, guess, or fabricate.
4. **Word limits are hard.** If a field exceeds its limit, truncate and note `[truncated]`.
5. **Empty fields are allowed but flagged.** If a required field cannot be extracted after one retry, write `""` (or `[]` for keywords) and add a `_warnings` field to the record.
